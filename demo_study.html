Study Summary

  • Total participants: 100
  • Trials per participant: 5
  • Questions per participant: 9
  • Study duration: ~5-7 minutes
  • Conditions: 2 modalities × 3 latency levels
  • Data collected: Quality ratings, perceived wait times, acceptance, preferences
