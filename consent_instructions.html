Response Latency Study - Consent & Instructions

Study Purpose

You will participate in a brief study about how response timing affects user experience. This should take approximately 5-7 minutes to complete.

What You'll Do

  1. 5 Question-Answer Trials: You'll see 5 different questions, each followed by an AI-generated response.
  2. Rate Each Response: After each response appears, you'll rate it on quality, wait time perception, and acceptability.
  3. Final Questions: You'll answer a few wrap-up questions about your overall experience.

Important Notes

  • Some responses may appear immediately, others may take a few seconds
  • Some responses may appear all at once, others may stream in gradually
  • Please pay attention to both the content and the timing of responses
  • There are no right or wrong answers - we want your honest perception

Consent

By clicking "Next" below, you consent to participate in this research study. Your responses will be kept anonymous and used only for research purposes. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.
